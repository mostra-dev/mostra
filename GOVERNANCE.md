# Governance

Mostra is a maintainer-led open-source project under the Apache-2.0 license.

## Roles

- **Maintainer.** Mirza Biçer ([@dev-mirzabicer](https://github.com/dev-mirzabicer)) leads the
  project: sets direction, accepts designs and work, reviews and merges pull requests, publishes
  releases and enforces the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Further maintainers join by the
  maintainer's invitation, and this file lists them.
- **Contributors.** Anyone who contributes under the [DCO](CONTRIBUTING.md). Agents that work on
  the repository follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and act under the maintainer's authorization.

## How decisions are made

- Design decisions are recorded in [docs/design/DECISIONS.md](docs/design/DECISIONS.md) with their
  source and rationale. That file is the authority on what was decided and why.
- Work is planned as work packages ([docs/design/WORK_PACKAGES.md](docs/design/WORK_PACKAGES.md)).
  The maintainer accepts each package, and acceptance authorizes its publication.
- Changes to an invariant ([docs/design/PRODUCT.md](docs/design/PRODUCT.md#rule-strengths)) need
  the maintainer's explicit approval and a decision record.
- Proposals from anyone are welcome as issues or discussions. The maintainer decides, and records
  significant decisions in the decision record.

## Licensing and trademarks

Everything users install, embed or self-host is Apache-2.0, and contributions use the DCO, not a
CLA. The name "Mostra" identifies this project; forks are welcome under a different name.
