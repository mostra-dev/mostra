A new package, `@mostra/themes`: Mostra's themes as data (`studio` and `mostra`, light and dark, checked for WCAG 2.2 AA by `defineTheme`), their stylesheets (`@mostra/themes/css/studio.css`, `css/mostra.css`) and self-hosted faces (Figtree, Jost, Spline Sans Mono), a template for the pages a server draws itself with no script (`createPages`), and one for mail (`renderMail`).
