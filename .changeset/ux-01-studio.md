Studio, the baseline stylesheet the Agent Skill gives artifacts (`mostra docs guide`), is now generated from `@mostra/themes` and has more of Mostra in it: Jost for headings and Figtree for the rest (with the system's faces as fallbacks), a warm pietra page ground with white cards, `popover` and `destructive-foreground` roles, and form-control edges at 3:1 contrast (WCAG 1.4.11).
