# MOSTRA_SHARE_NOT_FOUND

Unknown share. Severity: error. Phase: host.

## What it means

No share of this home has the ID given, or the artifact has no share.

## How to fix it

Use a share ID from `mostra share list`.

## Hint

List the home's shares with `mostra share list` and name one of them.
